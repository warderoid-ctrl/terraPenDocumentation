# Frequently asked questions

The terraPen is developed continuously, and its workflow differs from other plotters
on the market. These are the questions we hear most often.

For symptom-by-symptom help, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Is there an on/off switch?

No. The terraPen powers on as soon as it has power. Switch it off at the wall, or use
a switched extension lead or smart plug.

## Do I need a computer connected while it plots?

No. Artwork is stored on the terraPen's SD card, so once a plot starts you can close
the browser or take your laptop away.

## How big can I plot?

Just over A2.

## What software should I use?

[terraForge](terraForge.md) — our own application, built for this machine. The
terraPen plots standard G-code, so other tools work too; see
[Uploading files](uploadingFiles.md).

## What pens can I use?

Most pens fit, using the supplied bungs to match the barrel. Pen height matters far
more than pen brand — see [Your first plot](1stPlot.md#2-fit-the-pen-and-set-its-height).

## Why does it go into alarm after I cancel a plot?

Because the firmware no longer knows where the pen is. Clear the alarm with the
**Alarm** button or `$X`, then re-establish your origin.

## Can I control it over USB?

Partly. See [Connect via USB-C](ConnectTethered.md) — note the known limitations with
movement and pen up/down.

## Can I put it on my own Wi-Fi?

Yes — see [Connect to your own Wi-Fi network](connectToPersonalNetwork.md).
